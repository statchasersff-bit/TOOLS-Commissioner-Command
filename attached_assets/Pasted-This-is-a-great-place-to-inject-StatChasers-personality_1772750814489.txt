This is a great place to inject StatChasers personality. A lot of fantasy tools are sterile — yours can be data-driven but entertaining, which actually increases shareability and retention.

You already have the perfect inputs:

Power → current competitive strength

Dynasty Rank → long-term roster quality

Redraft Rank → current season strength

Draft Rank → future capital

So the Tier system should classify team archetypes, not just strength.

Below is a clean logic system + fun tier names.

Step 1 — Core Signals

Convert the ranks into signals.

For a 12-team league:

Strong

Rank 1-4

Middle

Rank 5-8

Weak

Rank 9-12

Define:

isContender = power <= 4 AND redraft <= 4
strongRoster = dynasty <= 4
futureLoaded = draft <= 4
futureDead = draft >= 9
weakNow = redraft >= 9
weakRoster = dynasty >= 9
Step 2 — Tier Categories

Instead of just “contender / rebuild”, use archetypes.

🏆 Tier 1 — Juggernaut

Definition

power <= 3
AND dynasty <= 4
AND redraft <= 4

Meaning

Best team now AND later.

Tooltip

League problem. Nobody wants to see this team in the playoffs.

💪 Tier 2 — Contender

Definition

power <= 4
AND redraft <= 6

Meaning

Strong playoff team.

Tooltip

Built to win this year.

📈 Tier 3 — Ascending Power

Definition

dynasty <= 4
AND power between 5–8

Meaning

Roster loaded but not dominating yet.

Tooltip

The future is bright. Probably a year early.

🎯 Tier 4 — Competitive

Definition

power 5–8
AND dynasty 5–8

Meaning

Solid but unspectacular.

Tooltip

Good roster, not scary.

🧠 Tier 5 — Rebuild (Smart)

Definition

weakNow
AND strongRoster
OR futureLoaded

Meaning:

Bad now but intentionally rebuilding.

Tooltip:

Playing the long game.

🎰 Tier 6 — Chaos Manager

Definition

dynasty 5–8
AND draft 5–8
AND power 9–12

Meaning:

Roster is messy and unclear direction.

Tooltip:

Nobody knows what the plan is.

🤡 Tier 7 — Taco

Definition

weakRoster
AND futureDead
AND power >= 9

Meaning:

Bad roster and no picks.

Tooltip:

The rebuild has not yet begun.

Step 7 — Code logic (what to tell Replit)

Give this to Replit AI:

Create a tier classification system for league teams using Power, Dynasty Rank, Redraft Rank, and Draft Rank.

For a 12-team league:

Strong rank = 1-4
Middle rank = 5-8
Weak rank = 9-12

Definitions:

Juggernaut
power <= 3 AND dynasty <= 4 AND redraft <= 4

Contender
power <= 4 AND redraft <= 6

Ascending Power
dynasty <= 4 AND power between 5 and 8

Competitive
power between 5 and 8 AND dynasty between 5 and 8

Strategic Rebuild
redraft >= 9 AND (dynasty <= 6 OR draft <= 4)

Directionless
power >= 9 AND dynasty between 5 and 8 AND draft between 5 and 8

League Taco
dynasty >= 9 AND draft >= 9 AND power >= 9

{
  tierName,
  tierColor,
  tooltip
}
