Important possibility

When Claude changed:

app.use(session(...))

to:

app.use("/api/sc-auth", session(...))

that was probably correct. But if your login route is not under /api/sc-auth and still expects session access, that could explain why login broke.

So have Claude check this immediately:

Is the login route under /api/sc-auth?

If login is currently something like:

/api/auth/login

but session middleware now only applies to:

/api/sc-auth/*

then login may be missing req.session.

The fix would be either:

app.use(["/api/sc-auth", "/api/auth"], sessionMiddleware)

or move/alias the login route under /api/sc-auth.

Do not re-enable global session middleware. That would bring back the pool starvation problem.
