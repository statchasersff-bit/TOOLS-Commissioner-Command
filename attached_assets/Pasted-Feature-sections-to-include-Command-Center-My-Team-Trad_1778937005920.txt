Content draft:

Command Center:
Your hub for managing teams, lineups, waivers, and priority actions across leagues.

My Team:
View your roster, team value, positional strengths, picks, and dynasty outlook.

Trade Center:
Analyze trades, find opportunities, sync active offers, message managers, and send offers directly through Sleeper.

Trade Finder:
Generates trade ideas based on team direction, roster needs, dynasty value, redraft value, age curves, and pick capital.

Trade Sync:
View incoming, outgoing, completed, rejected, and cancelled Sleeper trade offers. Accept, reject, withdraw, counter, edit, resend, and message trade partners. Requires Sleeper login.

Trade Calculator:
Build trades manually, compare both sides, include picks/FAAB, and send offers through Sleeper.

Trade Database:
Search and compare real trade examples and player values.

Lineup Command:
Review lineup issues, optimize starters, and submit lineup changes to Sleeper. Requires Sleeper login for saving changes.

Waiver Command:
Find available players, compare waiver targets, and submit claims through Sleeper. Requires Sleeper login.

Live Draft:
View draft boards, pick history, draft queue, recent picks, and live draft state. Sleeper login required for queue updates or making picks.

Share My Team:
Create a clean shareable snapshot of your roster, picks, team value, and dynasty profile.

Bulk Trade:
Build similar offers across multiple leagues and send them safely through a delayed queue. Requires Sleeper login.

Feedback & Bug Reports:
Send bug reports, feature requests, and UI feedback directly to StatChasers.

Sleeper Login / Privacy section:

Sleeper credentials are sent directly to Sleeper.
Dynasty Command uses Sleeper auth tokens to perform actions only when the user chooses.
Tokens should stay local/browser-side unless explicitly changed.
Never log JWTs or private DM contents.
